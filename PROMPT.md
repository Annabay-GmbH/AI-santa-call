# Santa Call: product brief

Create a mobile-first, open-source, supervised voice experience for children aged 3–12 and their trusted adults. The child chooses a language, speaks in short turns to a friendly AI Santa represented by a whimsical animated 3D character, and ends with an animated virtual gift, including a short name and message inspired by a harmless interest from the conversation. Screen all gift text before showing it, and never include identifying details. The app should be welcoming across cultures and family circumstances, easy to use with a grown-up, and usable with assistive technologies. Clearly explain that Santa and the voice are AI-generated and that the gift is virtual.

Design for minimal data collection: no child profiles, social features, advertising, or requests for identifying details. Keep credentials on the server, minimize retention, seek the necessary parental consent, and apply the appropriate child privacy rules and OpenAI data controls before real use. Use layered safeguards: a child-specific instruction, input and output checks, short sessions, a clear end control, a way to involve a trusted adult, testing in every supported language, and a human reporting and escalation process. If a child shares a concerning situation, respond compassionately and encourage finding a trusted adult rather than investigating, asking for details, or presenting Santa as an emergency service. Never ask a child to keep secrets, offer dangerous instructions, promise a physical gift, or shame them.

The implementation in this repository is an early prototype. The behavior prompt used by the server is in `server.js`; it is only one layer of safety and must be evaluated and revised before public launch. The launch requirements and current limitations are in the README.
